EarthQuake Watchdog helps you explore recent earthquake activity around the world through a clear, map-focused interface.

Browse the latest reported events, inspect their location and magnitude, and open detailed information for the earthquakes that matter to you. Flexible filters make it easier to focus on a time range, magnitude, or geographic area, while statistics provide a useful overview of recent seismic activity.

Features:
- Browse recently reported earthquakes worldwide
- Explore events on an interactive map
- View magnitude, location, depth, time, and event details
- Filter earthquake data to focus your search
- Review statistics and activity summaries
- Configure location and display preferences
- Use your device location or choose a location manually

Earthquake information is sourced from the U.S. Geological Survey (USGS). Data availability and update timing depend on the source service.

EarthQuake Watchdog is an informational tool. It is not an emergency warning system and should not be used as a substitute for official alerts, instructions, or advice from local authorities.
